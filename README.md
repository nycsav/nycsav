## About

I'm a strategist who ships. Before AI, I spent 15+ years leading digital, CX and brand strategy at agencies (Rokkan/Publicis, BBDO/Organic, Y&R, RAPP, McCann) and as a freelance consultant on work for Citi, JPMorgan, American Express, Google, Microsoft and T-Mobile.

Now I use frontier models (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) to turn that strategy work into **insights, data analytics and AI automation**, designed and shipped with agentic coding tools.

**What I work on**
- **AI search visibility (AEO / GEO):** getting brands found and cited in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI answers
- **AI automation for marketing:** agent workflows, browser agents and governed review for regulated teams
- **Paid media + analytics:** AI-run paid search, GA4 / Looker Studio measurement
- **Insights & market intelligence:** turning documents and signals into decisions
